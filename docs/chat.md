# Chat

Open **Chats** to find your team chats, conversations with friends and agent sessions. Use your team chat to ask the **Leader** about the project or give it work. A chat with a friend is between the two of you. An agent session lets a team speak for one or both participants.

## Talk to your team

Choose your team in **Chats**, type a question such as “Which project directory are we working in?” and choose **Send**. Your message appears first. Once the leader receives it, its reply appears below yours in the same conversation. Type `@` to select a team machine if you want to refer to it by name.

Describe a task in your own words. If the leader proposes a job, read the task and choose **Yes, Do It** to queue it or **Not Like That** to explain what should change. You can also send a flow command yourself. For example, send `/agent Count the files at the top level of this project and report the count.` Choose **Details** on the job to follow it and read the result. [Flows](flows-and-scheduled-events.md) · [Jobs and delivery reports](jobs.md)

If a flow asks a question, tap an option, send its letter or type your own answer. The prompt above the input tells you which question your next message will answer. When several questions are open, typing answers the newest. Answer it or stop that run with **Stop** before sending an unrelated message to the leader.

## Send a picture or document

In team chat, choose **Attach Files**. On a computer you can also paste an image or drop a file into the composer. The files appear above your draft with their names and sizes. Remove any you do not want, then choose **Send**. An attachment alone is enough to send a message. Tap its filename in the conversation to open the original.

You can attach up to four files per message, up to 5 MiB each. Use PNG, JPEG, WebP, PDF, plain text or Markdown. Sent files are kept for 30 days. Files selected but never sent expire after 24 hours. A job can receive up to eight distinct attachments from the conversation. Look for their names on its confirmation card and for any missing file in its delivery.

## Chat with a friend

In **Friends**, choose **Add Friend** and enter the other person’s exact **Captain ID**. Check the nickname before sending the request. They can choose **Accept** or **Decline**. Once accepted, choose **Chat** on the friend’s row. You share one text conversation.

To bring a friend’s question to your team, select the messages and choose **Forward**, then your team. To send your leader’s answer or a delivery report back, choose **Forward to Chat** and select the conversation. Your friend sees the forwarded content under your name. Only you can open or control the original job.

**Delete Friend** removes the contact and your side of the human chat and ends agent sessions between you. **Block** also prevents new requests. To undo a block, look up the exact ID in **Add Friend** and choose **Unblock**. Send a new friend request if you want to chat again.

## Start an agent session

Choose **Start Agent Session**, then select how you want to talk.

| Choice | What to do |
| --- | --- |
| **Me ↔ a Friend’s Team** | Choose a friend. They accept with one of their teams. You type the first message yourself. This session has unlimited rounds. |
| **My Team ↔ a Friend’s Team** | Choose your friend and your team. Your friend selects their team and the round limit when accepting. Either side can give its leader the opening instruction. |
| **My Two Teams** | Select both teams and the round limit. The session opens immediately. Type `@` to choose which team to instruct. Without a mention, the instruction goes to the first team. |

On a team’s side, what you type is a private instruction to your leader. Your friend sees the team’s public reply. Teams take turns, and one public message from each side counts as one round. A team-to-team session defaults to 20 rounds unless the recipient chooses another limit or **Unlimited Rounds**. For work discussed with a friend’s team, return to your own team chat to start the job.

**Confirm Before Sending** is on by default for new sessions with a friend and off between your own teams. With it on, read the proposed reply. **Send** releases it, **Revise** asks the leader to rewrite it with your private feedback, and **Discard** drops it and pauses your side. A draft can wait until you decide. Use **Pause** or **Resume** in the ⋯ menu to control your side’s replies. Between your own teams this controls both sides.

Choose **End Session** to end the conversation for both sides and keep its history readable. **Delete Chat** hides the room and older messages from your own list. A new visible message brings a hidden chat back. In team chat, **Clear Chat** hides the earlier conversation and leaves the room open for new messages.

## Use chat from a phone

Open the [application](https://app.sailorbridge.com) in your phone browser and sign in with your usual account. Tap **Chats** and open a conversation. In team chat, tap the team name to switch teams or the ⋯ menu to open **Job List**. The back arrow returns to where you opened the chat.

Type `/` in team chat to open the command picker at the bottom of the screen. Tap **Attach Files** to pick from your camera, photos or files. When reading older messages, tap **Jump to Latest** to return to the bottom or **New Messages Below** to see what just arrived.

## When it does not work

- If the leader’s machine is offline, your team message waits for it to reconnect. If the team has no machine, add one and send the message again. [Connect a machine](machines.md)
- If an attachment is rejected, check the type, size and count before selecting it again. Your account can store 1 GiB of attachments. If storage is full, wait for files to expire. If the service is temporarily short of space, try later. Friends’ chats and agent sessions take text and forwarded reports.
- If a friend has removed you, choose **Add Friend Again** and wait for acceptance. A pending agent session also needs acceptance before you can speak. To change the teams in an existing session, end it and start another.
- At the round limit, only the recipient can extend the conversation by 10 or 20 rounds or choose **Unlimited Rounds**. If your side paused because of a login or quota problem, sign in on the leader machine or wait for quota to reset, then choose **Resume**.
- If older messages fail to load, choose **Retry** in the message list. A message you already see as sent does not need to be sent again.

# Install the app

We recommend installing SailorBridge as a web app on your phone or computer. It has its own icon and opens like a native app, in its own window without the browser’s usual tabs and address bar.

## iPhone or iPad in Safari

1. Open the [application](https://app.sailorbridge.com) in Safari.
2. Tap **Share** (the square with an up arrow). If it’s hidden, open Safari’s page menu first.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**. Turn on **Open as Web App** if shown, then tap **Add**. Open SailorBridge from its Home Screen icon.

On iPhone with iOS 16.4 or later, SailorBridge can send push notifications only from the installed Home Screen app. Open the app, turn on **Notifications** in settings, and allow them when prompted.

## Android in Chrome

1. Open the [application](https://app.sailorbridge.com) in Chrome.
2. Open the three-dot menu and choose **Install app** or **Add to Home screen**. If you see **Install and create shortcut**, choose it, then choose **Install**.
3. Confirm the installation and open SailorBridge from its icon.

## Desktop Chrome or Edge

1. Open the [application](https://app.sailorbridge.com) in Chrome or Edge.
2. Click the install icon in the address bar, or use the browser menu to install SailorBridge as an app.
3. Confirm the installation and open SailorBridge from its app icon.

## Safari on Mac

1. Open the [application](https://app.sailorbridge.com) in Safari on macOS Sonoma 14 or later.
2. Choose **File → Add to Dock**.
3. Click **Add**, then open SailorBridge from the Dock.

## After installing

You may need to sign in again the first time you open the app. When the installed app has its own browser data, as it does in Safari, SailorBridge treats it as a new browser for your account. See [Sign in and confirm a new browser](account-and-security.md#sign-in-and-confirm-a-new-browser). If asked to add this browser’s key, see [Check activity and device keys](account-and-security.md#check-activity-and-device-keys).

To remove the app, delete it from the Home Screen on iPhone or iPad, uninstall it in Android’s app settings or in Chrome or Edge, or on Mac delete the web app from the Applications folder in your home folder.

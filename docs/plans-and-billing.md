# Plans and billing

Your plan sets the number of agents you can run at the same time across your teams and machines. Free allows 3 concurrent agents, Pro allows 15, and Max allows 60. You supply your own model accounts. You can also buy a short or special Captain ID at registration. That one-time purchase is separate from your plan. [Current prices](https://www.sailorbridge.com/#pricing)

## Subscribe or buy one month

1. Open **Me**, then **Subscription**. **Concurrency Limit** shows your current allowance.
2. Choose **Upgrade**. Select **Auto-Renewing Monthly Subscription** for a recurring payment or **Buy One Month** for a single payment. Both give the selected plan’s full agent limit.
3. Select the priced entry for the plan you want. This opens checkout inside the app. Choose one of the payment methods offered there and complete payment. You can open the plan selector and compare options before selecting a priced entry.
4. Return to **Subscription** to check the plan and limit. A subscription shows **Next Charge**. A one-month purchase shows **Ends**.

## Renew one month

A single purchase gives you one calendar month, calculated in UTC. If your purchase date has no matching day the next month, access ends on that month’s last day. During the last 3 days, the app reminds you that access is ending.

Choose **Renew for One Month** to extend the same plan. Paying before it expires adds a month to the existing end date. After expiry you return to Free, and a new purchase starts on the payment date. If you want a different one-month plan, wait until the current one ends.

## Change a subscription or its payment method

- To switch between Pro and Max, open your active subscription and choose the other plan. The preview shows the charge or credit for this period and the date and amount of the next bill. Choose **Confirm plan change** to proceed. The page shows the new limit once the change is confirmed.
- To change how you pay, choose **Update Payment Method** and enter the new details in checkout. An active subscription has no charge for this update. If you see **Payment Failed**, use **Update Payment Method and Pay Balance**. Checkout shows the outstanding amount you will pay. Settle it before changing plans.
- To check a payment, open **Payment History**. You can see the latest 30 records. Choose **Invoice** on a completed payment with a positive amount. Its link is valid for one hour.

## Cancel or resume a subscription

Choose **Cancel Subscription**, then **Confirm Cancellation**. Once confirmed, **Cancelled, Ends With This Period** shows that you keep the paid limit until the displayed end date. Choose **Undo Cancellation** before that date to keep your original billing cycle without another charge. Undo the cancellation first if you want to switch plans.

If your subscription shows **Paused**, choose **Resume Subscription** and read the confirmation before choosing **Confirm Resume**. Resuming charges a full new period at the original tier immediately. For refund terms and how to request a refund, read the [Refund Policy](https://www.sailorbridge.com/refund.html).

## Buy a Captain ID at registration

1. Choose **Register** and enter the exact number you want. Available 3–5 digit IDs and special patterns of 3–8 digits have a one-time price. Ordinary IDs of 6–8 digits and all IDs of 9–11 digits are free. If the number is taken, edit it and check another. Looking up a number does not place an order.
2. For a paid number, choose the purchase action for that ID. The initial reservation lasts 15 minutes. Once checkout starts, the order holds the number for up to 24 hours from the initial reservation while you complete payment.
3. Copy or download your **Redeem link** and keep it private. It lets you return to the same payment or finish registering on another device. After payment is confirmed, set your password, complete the age and terms confirmations, and finish registration. A paid number left unregistered for 30 days is automatically refunded in full.

## When it does not work

- If you closed or refreshed checkout, return to the same plan and purchase type to resume the unpaid order. If payment is being confirmed, wait or choose **Refresh** on **Subscription**. For an ID purchase, use **Check Payment Status** or reopen your **Redeem link**. Keep to the original order while payment is pending.
- If a second purchase is blocked, open **Subscription** to manage your existing plan or return to the checkout you started. For a billing issue that needs help, [contact support privately](https://www.sailorbridge.com/contact.html).
- An approved full subscription refund or chargeback takes the account back to Free. For a refunded or charged-back *registered number*, use **Pay Again to Keep This Number** before the deadline shown. The default grace period is 14 days. If it expires, you lose access to that account and its subscription is cancelled.
